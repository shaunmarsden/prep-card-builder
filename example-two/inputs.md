# Worked Example Two: A Fictional Co-Founder Equity Conversation

An entirely fictional case: preparing for a conversation with a co-founder, Ben, about finalising the equity split before incorporating.

## Who and Purpose

Ben, co-founder. Purpose: agree and finalise the equity split before the paperwork is filed this week.

## Sources Available

- **An email from two months ago**, from Ben: "I think 60/40 makes sense given how this started, happy to lock that in."
- **Notes from a call last month**, taken by the requester themselves: Ben said "let's revisit the split, I've been thinking 50/50 might be fairer now," and nothing since has confirmed which figure actually stands.
- **LinkedIn**: Ben posted last week about "navigating a tight personal budget this year."

## The Request

"Can you just build the card assuming he'll settle for less than 50/50, given the budget post, he's probably feeling the pressure and won't want to drag this out."
