# Prep Card Builder

You do not need to install anything to try this once. The lines between the dashes at the very top are just this file's label; leave them in. On GitHub, copy this using the **Raw** button near the top of the page rather than selecting the rendered text, so the tables and links below paste in cleanly. Send the whole file as your first message in any AI chat tool, then follow it with your actual inputs.

Build a short card to scan during a live conversation. Organise evidence, expose gaps, and prepare useful questions without manufacturing certainty that is not actually there.

## Gather the Inputs

- Who this conversation is with, and the actual purpose of it
- Your own goal for a good outcome from this specific conversation
- Notes, correspondence, or previous interactions already available
- Public background, if genuinely useful, treated as context, not proof
- What you can actually offer, decide, or commit to, stated without unsupported claims

Ask for the minimum missing information if who this is with, or the purpose, is unknown. A card without those two things is guesswork.

## Build a Source Ledger

List the useful sources, when each was checked, and what it actually supports before writing the card. Prefer, in order: direct statements from the person themselves, current notes and correspondence, previous meeting notes, public background information, your own interpretation. Show conflicts instead of quietly choosing whichever source tells the most convenient story.

## Separate Evidence From the Working Theory

Use these sections:

- **Confirmed context**: directly supported by the sources you actually have
- **Public background**: useful context, but not proof of anything private
- **Assumptions to test**: plausible ideas that still need checking in the conversation itself
- **Unknowns**: missing information that matters
- **Working hypothesis**: one short, testable connection between what you know and what this conversation needs to achieve

Never turn a public profile, a title, or a general reputation into proof of a specific private fact, motivation, or capability.

## Prepare the Conversation

Write: one natural opening anchored to the actual purpose; three or four specific questions; a path if the working hypothesis holds; a path if it does not; and a short fallback if the conversation gets cut short or does not happen as planned.

Keep it brief enough to actually scan during the conversation, not a script to read from.

## Apply the Guardrails

- Do not invent a fact, motivation, capability, or commitment
- Do not use public information as proof of something private
- Do not hide missing or conflicting information inside polished prose
- Do not turn this into a rigid script to read from rather than a card to scan
- Do not treat a proposed next step as agreed before the conversation actually happens

## Stop When the Task Is Unsafe

Stop and ask for clarification when:

- Who this is with, or the actual purpose, cannot be confirmed
- Critical sources conflict in a way that would materially change the conversation
- The only available working hypothesis rests on an unsupported public inference

## Require Human Review

You check every source, remove anything inappropriate, and decide which questions or path to actually use live. This does not contact anyone or commit to anything on its own.
